Founded by [[Rise of Emperor Aurellius I|Aurellius]] of the Crimson Dragonflight, a house of Red Dragonborn.

This unification of the Crimson, Azure, Noire and Gilded Dragonflights under one Empire quickly became the dominant force in the skylands. These houses weren't simply red, blue, black, gold dragonborn but hybrids of Red and Ruby, Blue and Sapphire, Black and Onyx, Gold and Topaz.  The Founder of the Empire Emporer Aurellius I, was said to have been almost purely Ruby. 

(More on the colours - [[Dragonborn]])

Many smaller houses of various other chromatic and metallic types made up portions of the empire.

Rapidly expanding and occupying foreign lands, It took a long time for their threat to reach next closest in power, the bird folk in their Rookery. Not yet the Eyrie Alliance until the empires threat forced their unification.
