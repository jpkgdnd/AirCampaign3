The dwarves are known for their mustards, but its kinda a pepper based hot sauce and seedy mustard combo The birds also love mustard but non sauced, just the seeds 

The consortium popularised a mayolike sauce, they aerate it so its more like a creamy foam. Eating non aerated mayo would be seen as like eating uncooked pasta or frozen toast. 

The tabaxi have a condiment thats kind of a sour hot sauce minus the spice, it mostly goes on soups like a dash of yoghurt on a curry, theyre the curry makers of this world, forced to make do with bad meat and rats while enslaved by the Birdfolk for centuries  

Mousefolk invented the closest thing to ketchup, but its berry based and quite sweet and vinegary Custard is universal and beloved by all, its the ketchup of the dessert world in the skylands
