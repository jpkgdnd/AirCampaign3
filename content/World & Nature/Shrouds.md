The shrouds are pockets of space between the flight paths which harbour wild skylands, ancient debris and is known to be the lands of [[Dragons]]. The shrouds are the most treacherous places in the skylands, only in very modern times are they realistically explorable due to the advancements in engine technology. A thick layer of cloud and wind make entering the shroud an expensive task, a strong ship and crew along with the elemental cores of the engine working overtime, consuming a great many motes to break through the shrouds.
