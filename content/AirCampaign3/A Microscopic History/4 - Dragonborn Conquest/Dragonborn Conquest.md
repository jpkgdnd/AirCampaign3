The [[Draconic Empire]] lead by Emperor Aurelius IV begins its conquest across the skylands. Conquering and staking claim to a lot of land both occupied and not. Devastating many [[Elf]] settlements. 

Expansion from the [[The Grubfoot Coalition]], the [[Elf]] people. All lead to a brutal retaliation.

The Conquest culminates with a large portion of the skylands being under control of various dragonflights within the Draconic Empire.  Much of the Elven tribes are now wiped out of under control, many grubfoot frontier settlements taken and now operated by the empire. Many natives across the skylands are now under Draconic Foot. [[Goliath]] [[Orc]] [[Beastfolk]] [[Halfling]] [[Tort]] all have portions of their population within the empires borders.

A rogue [[Birdfolk]] [[Pirates]] by the name of Ryker, the Squak. Was hired by the empire to begin instigating conflict  on their now touching borders, this would lead to the eventual war between [[Eyrie Alliance]] and the Draconic Empire.
