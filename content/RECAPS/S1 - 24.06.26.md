## Recap 
[[Venetim Vanathros]] arrived at the underdock of [[The Grand Consortium]] alongside his loyal Bosun, [[Swain]]. On the run from.. something, they arrived and were greeted by a [[Beastfolk]] by the name of [[Percy Trotini]], member of [[Underbelly Familia]]. Percy recognised the airship, a family airship, but not the man that seemed to own it, this was enough information to make their arrival smell like opportunity. Percy offered Venetim a fresh coat of pain, a small crew and enough fuel for the road, landing on a cost of around 6000 gold. In the form of an IOU.

Penniless the pair went up to find a job at the [[Freelancers Guild]], bad timing left one job left, a missing person in the still shroud to the south east. Bounty provided by friends, this person seemed to have gone MIA during an archaeological dig. This job was of interest to another however, its location happened to be in the region where a [[Draconic Warlords]] was operating. [[Rexus Vakarim]] laid in wait for a crew to take the job, quickly joining up to seek his vengeance.

When they got back to the underdock a variety of strange fellows were waiting, a crew to be selected. A Pilot, Lookout and navigator for the journey to come. [[Squark Skylark]] seemingly wasn't looking for work, more having work put upon him, a debt with the Familia(?).  Percy waved them off hoping for a return on his investment.

After a relatively safe flight, the crew arrived at the location this archaeologist was supposedly heading to. A tiny island not much bigger than the length of the airship, a caved in pit was all that stood there. A voice within cried out for help at the sound of people above. [[Baxter]] emerged having spent weeks within these small ruins, a message of unknown language taunting him day and night. Goblins from the GrinkGob Nation sprung out from the bushes, making a dramatic entrance with a jetpack the gang were too flustered to ask why they're there or anything like that. The Goblins didn't last long. After the dust settled they had time to look around, some old stone fragment of an artefact laid in the dirt, alongside a blue silken armband. After a few hours of searching the area, they decided to head back to the consortium.

## TLDR 
Venetim, Swain on ship arrive at Consortium

5000 gold debt from Percy Trotini to get ship operational, hire crew & Squark

Missing person job in still shroud, find Rexus wanting to go to those parts

Find Baxter (missing person) in a cave in, strange markings on wall, strange fragment of something, evidence of a Azure Dragonflight Warlord. Baxter confused as to where he is.
