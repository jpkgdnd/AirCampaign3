
The crew arrived at the location of the observation post construction site, a high hill in the north west still shroud, their contract was complete but they spotted an old metallic door embedded in nearby woodland.

  This ancient site turned out to be something like a crypt, but closer to a morgue with a surgery room, elemental mephits roamed the ancient halls and many traps made it a dangerous excursion. After this, the crew did some charting and while flying around encountered a pack of skysharks which were swiftly dealt with.

  
The next stop was made to deliver Mr Perry Fairweather to his destination, equipped with a canister full of motes, several weather devices and a ritual site, the gang left him to his business.

Next was the search for a downed prisoner transport ship, the wreckage was found quickly and was a gruesome scene of a Griffon and its young feasting on the corpses of the Eyrie crew. Baxter, potentially suffering a concussion from the several near death experiences of the previous day, rushed to kill the children and mother on the opposite side of the crash. The ships Elebox was retrieved as per the contract while Venetim and Squark followed a trail away from the ship to find survivors. The blue dragonborn escaped prisoners were held up in the cave, some convoluted trickery and ill-fated combat later, Venetim, and one of the other remained.

A probe of his mind revealed that the ship was subject to a prisoner mutiny and one of the escapees believed their warlord leader, unnamed at this time, caused it, the other did not.

They were retaken as prisoners and returned to the ship before heading back in the direction of Perry, as the crew approached his last known location they noticed a concentration of storm clouds, lightning cracking toward one spot.

When arrived they saw the equipment he carried was setup all around a cannister of sorts being struck by lightning. The cannister was full of motes, and Perry intended to release his late wives ashes into the mote infused concentrated storm. The party stood and watched as an air elemental infused with the banshee of Perrys dead wife took its form and lunged at the gnome rendering him unconscious. Its fury didnt end there as it lashed out at the crew who stood between it and Perry. She was put to rest and the sleeping Perry was returned to the ship. He does not yet know of his elemental wifes fate.
