After years of grueling conflict with the [[Eyrie Alliance]], the [[Tabaxi]],[[Mousefolk]],[[Kobold]],[[Beastfolk]] rebellion settled with the Eyrie. Granted generous citizen rights almost identical to [[Birdfolk]] themselves, minus rights to hold certain political or military high office. 

Pressure for this deal mostly came from the Eyries conflict with the [[Draconic Empire]] during the [[Dragonborn Conquest]]. The Eyrie needed troops. But even after the conflict ended, things went on as they now were. Despite this, the rebel species still hold bad blood for the [[Birdfolk]].
