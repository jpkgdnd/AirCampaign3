A long and grueling war leaves both sides significantly worse off, the [[Draconic Empire]] is left in pieces. In the modern day [[Draconic Warlords]] are all that remain, holding out in various places and sizes. 

After the war is won the [[Eyrie Alliance]] continue to hunt down the larger splinters of the former empire, but they are spread thin giving way to [[The Great Springs]] and [[Rebellion Settlement]].
