The decades that followed on the Humans archipelago were filled with civil war.

Commonfolk, Nobles, Merchants fled the kingdom over the course of these wars eventually settling new lands

These new lands they settled were just the easiest to reach, peaceful, unoccupied land they could find, which turned out to be their greatest fortune. The settlement that grew from this eventually become the hub known as [[The Grand Consortium]]

## Dragons Attack & Response
The wars were brought to a halt by a devastating attack from Wild [[Dragons]], unifiying what remained of the [[Human Kingdoms]].

Three Kings remained after the dragon attacks, uniting their armies into a defence force and establishing a guild to deal with the wild dragon threat, [[The Wyrm Wardens]].
