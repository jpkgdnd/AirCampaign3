[[The Grubfoot Coalition]] establishes their first mining colony on a nearby sky island.

## Airship Invention
A dwarf by the name of Strongstride discovered an ancient ruin which contained an ancient artifact, [[elementals]] bound within cylinders, seemingly made to slot into devices.

The Grubfoot engineers designed a new version of an airship, something they had desperately been trying to perfect for decades now. This one was powered by the ancient cores they found, Water and Fire [[elementals]] bound and utilized in an engine to create massive steam power.

On the day they finished this task a [[Goblin]] by the name of [[Grinkle]] infiltrated the skydock and made off with the plans for the airship. Sowing the Seed of the Goblins greatest advancement.

## Colony founded
Early in their explorations the grubfoot colonisers discovered a new land of volcanic peaks and rich rocky bellies. Naturally Inhabited by the easy going [[Tort]] they quickly allied themselves with them and began exploiting the land. Ores, minerals and fire [[Elementals]] in abundance.
