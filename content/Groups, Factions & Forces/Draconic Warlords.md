After the fall of Acurvus, the capitol of the [[Draconic Empire]], 22 of the empires generals locations and status were unknown to the leaders of the attack, the [[Eyrie Alliance]]. The rest either killed, captured or surrendered following the declaration of victory.

Following that declaration, due to the length of the conflict, political pressure from the population of the Eyrie demanded a major scaling back of military commitments. Drafted forces returned home and the core military were left to deal with the unwavering remaining warlords, some still in their old territories occupying land, some moved nomadically around the skylands, raiding to survive.
