The Guild was founded in an attempt to fight piracy. Heavily funded by [[The Grubfoot Coalition]] and [[Human Kingdoms]] who most suffered at the hands of pirates. 

Now a hub for all sorts of work, exploring ruins, missing persons, body guarding etc etc. Jobs can come from both citizen and the state where needed and is available to all. Some state-sponsored work requires a certain level of trust from the guild in sensitive cases.
