The land this place was built upon, at an opportune crossroads of airship travel routes, is mostly obscurred now by the towering mass of The Grand Consortium. Cluttered towers of ship yards, workshops, markets, depots, ports fill the airspace. Luxury villas and apartments occupy the upper limits of these structures.

The underside of the Grand Consortium, in the shadows of the structures above makes home to the underbelly. Gangs and mobs vying for control over various streets and establishments. Most prominently the [[Underbelly Familia]] run most of the underbelly.

Tourism thrives from [[Birdfolk]],[[Humans]],[[Dwarf]],[[Gnome]],[[Tort]],[[Goliath]] and [[Orc]] - in the early days many [[Elf]] seek work at the consortium to escape the harsh nature of their nomadic homelands. 

[[Dragonborn]] often separate to the empire can be seen here also. [[The Grand Consortium]] becomes a home to all. The Grand Consortium Sponsors various ventures in the land, [[The Grand College of Daring Explorers]] the shipyards which make it possible for the GC to operate as a hub for trade and tourism.

In the modern day [[Tabaxi]] , [[Mousefolk]] , [[Kobold]] and various other [[Beastfolk]] frequent the Consortium too.
