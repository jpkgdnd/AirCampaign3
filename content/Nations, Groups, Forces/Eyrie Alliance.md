Founded by an alliance of various [[Birdfolk]] species. The Eyrie Alliance history is stained by slavery and territorial aggression.

In the modern day it is seen by many as a force of good, the [[Elf]] people that were freed from the [[Draconic Empire]] have a complex relationship having settled in Eyrie land unwelcomed. [[Goliath]],[[Orc]],[[Humans]] and everyone freed from the empire tend to view them favourably.

However the [[Tabaxi]], [[Mousefolk]],[[Kobold]] and [[Beastfolk]] would likely have a different view
