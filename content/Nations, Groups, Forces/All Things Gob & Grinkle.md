## Grinkle

A concept. A mantle. An Idea

Passed down through generations of goblins, the legend of Grinkle is vast in scope and vague in truth. What is known for sure is there once lived a goblin named Grinkle who obtained the information needed to see goblinkind soar into the sky.

Since that day, many goblins have held the title of Grinkle, sometimes several at a time. All aligned in one idea, the advancement of goblinkind.

## GrinkGob Nation

In the modern day, A group of [[Goblin]]s following the ways of the Grinkle have organized and become a prominent threat in the skylands. No longer following a single Grinkle, GrinkGob nation believes all gobs are grinks and all grinks are gobs and that grink is a state of mind for the gob.

This group is focussed on stealing technology from various groups, primarily [[The Grubfoot Coalition]] and they operate out of small hideouts and coves known only to them.

## The Church of Grinkle

Around the time that Grinkgob nation split off into an identifiable faction, also emerged The Church of Grinkle. Not subscribing to the concept that Grinkle is a concept or an idea. But a divine being, blessing goblinkind with technology and the means to take more. The church believes they must continue the work of Grinkle and that enlightenment can only be reached through technosupremacy and transgoblinism.

## The Grinkle Herasy

Much larger in population is the remaining portion of Goblinkind, hungry, ostracized for their association with the church and nation. Trying to get by in the small islands and uncolonized corners of the skylands. Some find work in the underbelly of [[The Grand Consortium]], used for cheap labour.
