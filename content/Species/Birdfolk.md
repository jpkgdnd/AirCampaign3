[[Birdfolk]]

Make up of a large variety of subspecies the birdfolk are natural skylanders.

## [Rooks] - Crow, Raven, Magpie ...
Rooks make up a significant portion of the birdfolk population, Crow, Magpie, Ravens are the primary groups. They take part in all parts of the [[Eyrie Alliance]] but are very adept rogues, spies, plotters and schemers.

## Raptors - Hawks, Falcons, Eagles
Smaller in numbers but some of the most powerful of the birdfolk, Hawks, Falcons and Eagles rose to the top of the birdfolk ladder naturally, much larger and stronger than most others they quickly became the ones people turned to in matters of military and representing the [[Eyrie Alliance]] when a firm hand was needed.

## Wildfowl - Swans, Ducks, Geese
Natural leaders the swans and geese make up large portions of the government and military leadership within the Eyrie Alliance. Ducks on the other hand are more common folk, farmers and traders.

## Cranes & Herons
Cranes are the mystics of birdfolk society, meditative and wise, often consulted in dire times by the other birdfolk. Often living in remote marshlands away from civilisation.

Herons are also a wise bunch but a bit more present in birdfolk society and the Eyrie Alliance. Munch of the Eyries wizarding population is made up of Herons. Many dedicate themselves to philosophy and politics alongside the wildfowl.

## Owls
The wise owls are most often seen in courts as judges and lawmen, operating branches of the Eyries government. Few in numbers but hard to miss in all walks of birdfolk life.

## Parrot
One of the only birdfolk species almost entirely absent from the modern day Eyrie Alliance, they often have the gift of the gab, keen memories and fiercely independent and individualistic.
