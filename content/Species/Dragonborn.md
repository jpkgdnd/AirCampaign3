History has forgotten the origins of the Dragonborn, their ties to the [[Dragons]] often speculated upon. Despite their similar natures, the Dragonborn and its [[Draconic Empire]] of the past were never exempt from the ire of the wild dragons.

Dragonborn come in all sorts of colours, all categorized into Chromatic or Metallic. But a rare few are blessed with naturally occurring Gem features. Seen by the dragonborn as a sign of power or purity, Red Dragonborn may be blessed with ruby features, blue with sapphire, green and emerald, gold and topaz, black and onyx... Rarely seen and even more rarely seen in abundance. Rumours say the original [[Rise of Emperor Aurellius I]] was because Aurellius was almost entirely Ruby Scaled, giving him the way needed to unite the dragonflights under one banner.
